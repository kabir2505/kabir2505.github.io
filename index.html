# Hi, I'm Kabir J.

I'm a Research Intern at Microsoft Research, working on embeddings, and a Data Science student at IIT Madras.

Before this, I worked on interpretability in multimodal LLMs at Precog, IIIT Hyderabad with Prof. Ponnurangam Kumaraguru, and on self-supervised learning for 3D medical imaging at VISTA Lab, IISc Bangalore, which became an IJCAI-ECAI 2026 paper.

Broadly, I want to understand how models think, reason and fail, and how they can be improved. That draws me to interpretability, alignment, pretraining, self-supervised learning and grounding in multimodal models. I like learning methods by building them from scratch, which is how this page ended up with a neural network training in the next pane.

1:train — python train_simclr.py --tau 0.20 training
step 0 InfoNCE — align — uniform —
or type train --tau 0.1

A 16→32→2 MLP learning with a contrastive (SimCLR) loss on unlabeled points, live on your CPU. It never sees the colors; they're hidden concepts shown only to you. Once it converges, the clusters draw a Vinland Saga Vegvísir. Hover a point to see its two augmented views.

self_supervised_learning:Learning useful representations from unlabeled data, like the contrastive model training in pane 1.
