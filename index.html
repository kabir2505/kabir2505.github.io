Hi, I'm Kabir J.

I'm a Research Intern at Microsoft Research, working on embeddings, and a Data Science student at IIT Madras.

Before this, I worked on interpretability in multimodal LLMs at Precog, IIIT Hyderabad with Prof. Ponnurangam Kumaraguru, and on self-supervised learning for 3D medical imaging at VISTA Lab, IISc Bangalore, which became an IJCAI-ECAI 2026 paper.

Broadly, I want to understand how models think, reason and fail, and how they can be improved. That draws me to interpretability, alignment, pretraining, self-supervised learning and grounding in multimodal models. I like learning methods by building them from scratch, which is how this page ended up with a neural network training on it.

02Research interests

interpretability
Understanding what models represent internally and why they behave the way they do: features, circuits, and how they fail.
alignment
Making models reliably do what we intend, and noticing early when they don't.
pretraining
How data and objectives during pretraining shape the capabilities and representations of LLMs and MLLMs.
self-supervised learning
Learning useful representations from unlabeled data, like the contrastive model training on this page.
grounding
How multimodal models tie language to what is actually in an image, and what they rely on when they talk about it.
